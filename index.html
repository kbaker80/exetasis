Exetasis

Every night, the day gets judged.

A nightly habit tracker for Android. At the hour you choose it asks how the day went, one question at a time, scores it out of 100 against a list you control, and tells you plainly what it thinks - against your target and against your own average. Exetasis is Greek for an examination: every night, the day sits one.

Everything stays on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics.

£2.99, once, on Google Play (United Kingdom). No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads.
